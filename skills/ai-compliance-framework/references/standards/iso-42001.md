# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI Management System Standard

> **Status:** Published international standard
> **Binding:** Voluntary — but increasingly required in procurement and certification contexts
> **Confidence:** HIGH for structure and clause topics; MEDIUM for exact sub-clause wording (ISO standards are copyrighted — full text requires purchase)
> **Source:** <https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html>

## Overview

ISO/IEC 42001 is the world's first international standard for AI management systems (AIMS). Modelled on ISO management system structure (Annex SL), it provides a framework for organisations developing, providing, or using AI systems.

Organisations can be **certified** to ISO 42001 by accredited certification bodies.

## Structure (Annex SL Clauses)

| Clause | Title | AI-Specific Focus |
| -------- | ------- | ------------------- |
| 4 | Context of the Organisation | AI system inventory, interested parties, scope |
| 5 | Leadership | Top management commitment, AI policy, roles |
| 6 | Planning | AI risk assessment, objectives, change planning |
| 7 | Support | Resources, competence, awareness, communication, documented information |
| 8 | Operation | AI system lifecycle management, impact assessment, data management |
| 9 | Performance Evaluation | Monitoring, internal audit, management review |
| 10 | Improvement | Nonconformity, corrective action, continual improvement |

## Key Requirements by Clause

### Clause 4: Context

- Determine external/internal issues relevant to AI (regulatory, ethical, societal, technical)
- Identify interested parties and their requirements (users, regulators, affected communities)
- Define AIMS scope (which AI systems, which business units)
- Maintain AI system inventory

### Clause 5: Leadership

- Top management demonstrates commitment to responsible AI
- AI policy established, communicated, and maintained
- Roles and responsibilities for AI governance defined and assigned
- AI policy addresses: responsible development, transparency, fairness, accountability, safety

### Clause 6: Planning

- AI risk assessment methodology defined
- Risks and opportunities identified for each AI system
- AI objectives set (measurable, time-bound)
- Plans to achieve objectives documented

### Clause 7: Support

- Adequate resources for AIMS (people, infrastructure, tools)
- Competence requirements for AI roles (developers, validators, operators)
- Awareness programme for all relevant personnel
- Internal and external communication processes for AI governance

### Clause 8: Operation (AI-Specific)

- **8.2 AI Risk Assessment:** Systematic assessment of risks from AI systems
- **8.3 AI Risk Treatment:** Selection and implementation of risk controls
- **8.4 AI System Impact Assessment:** Assessment of potential impacts on individuals and society
- **8.5 AI System Lifecycle:** Requirements across development, deployment, operation, retirement
- **8.6 Data Management:** Data quality, provenance, consent, retention for AI
- **8.7 Third-party and Supply Chain:** Managing AI risks from suppliers and partners

### Clause 9: Performance Evaluation

- Monitor and measure AI system performance
- Internal audits of AIMS at planned intervals
- Management review of AIMS effectiveness

### Clause 10: Improvement

- React to nonconformities and take corrective action
- Continual improvement of AIMS suitability, adequacy, effectiveness

## Annex A Controls (Normative)

ISO 42001 Annex A provides a set of controls that organisations must consider:

| Control Area | Key Controls |
| ------------- | ------------- |
| **A.2 Policies** | AI governance policy, responsible AI principles |
| **A.3 Internal organisation** | Roles, responsibilities, competence |
| **A.4 Resources** | Data, tooling, computing resources |
| **A.5 Assessing impacts** | Impact assessment methodology, review process |
| **A.6 AI system lifecycle** | Requirements, design, verification, validation, deployment, operation, retirement |
| **A.7 Data** | Data provenance, quality, preparation, acquisition |
| **A.8 Information** | Documentation, record-keeping, communication |
| **A.9 Use** | Terms of use, responsible use policies |
| **A.10 Third parties** | Supplier management, outsourcing controls |

## Annex B Guidance (Informative)

Provides implementation guidance for each Annex A control. Key guidance areas:

- AI-specific risk assessment techniques
- Impact assessment for AI systems
- Data management for AI (lifecycle, quality, bias)
- AI system documentation requirements
- Monitoring and measurement approaches

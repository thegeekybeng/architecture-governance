# Japan — AI Governance

> **Status:** Active guidance (voluntary)
> **Binding:** NO at framework level; APPI (data protection) is binding
> **Confidence:** HIGH for existence; MEDIUM for specific guideline details (some documents Japanese-only)
> **Sources:**
>
> - Social Principles of Human-Centric AI (Cabinet Decision, 2019)
> - AI Guidelines for Business (METI, 2024)
> - AI Governance Guidelines v1.1 (METI, 2022)

## Social Principles of Human-Centric AI (7 Principles)

| # | Principle | Focus |
| --- | ----------- | ------- |
| 1 | **Human-centric** | AI must not infringe on fundamental human rights |
| 2 | **Education and literacy** | Promote AI literacy across society |
| 3 | **Privacy protection** | Personal data handled appropriately per APPI |
| 4 | **Ensuring security** | Robustness and cybersecurity |
| 5 | **Fair competition** | Prevent monopolisation, ensure market openness |
| 6 | **Fairness, accountability, transparency** | Non-discriminatory, accountable, transparent |
| 7 | **Innovation** | Balance regulation with innovation promotion |

## AI Guidelines for Business (METI, 2024)

Practical guidance for companies across the AI value chain. Covers:

- AI developers: Training data governance, model documentation, safety testing
- AI providers: Transparency to users, monitoring, incident response
- AI users: Appropriate use, human oversight, feedback mechanisms

## Data Protection: APPI (Act on Protection of Personal Information)

- Consent required for collection and use of personal information
- Purpose of use must be specified and limited
- Sensitive personal information (race, creed, social status, medical history, criminal record) requires explicit consent
- Cross-border transfer restrictions — adequacy, consent, or equivalent protection
- PPC (Personal Information Protection Commission) is the supervisory authority
- Mandatory breach notification effective April 2022

## Hiroshima AI Process (G7, 2023)

Japan-led G7 initiative establishing international guiding principles for advanced AI:

- 11 guiding principles for AI developers
- Code of conduct for organisations developing advanced AI
- Focus on foundation models and generative AI
- Voluntary commitments from major AI companies

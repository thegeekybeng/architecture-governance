# ASEAN — Guide on AI Governance and Ethics

> **Status:** Active guidance (voluntary)
> **Binding:** NO — non-binding guide for ASEAN member states
> **Confidence:** HIGH
> **Source:** <https://asean.org/asean-guide-on-ai-governance-and-ethics/>
> **Published:** February 2024

## Overview

The ASEAN Guide provides a practical, non-binding framework for AI governance across the 10 ASEAN member states. It is explicitly aligned with the OECD AI Principles and Singapore's Model AI Governance Framework.

## Seven AI Governance Principles

| # | Principle | Description |
| --- | ----------- | ------------- |
| 1 | **Transparency and Explainability** | Disclose AI use; provide accessible explanations |
| 2 | **Fairness and Equity** | Non-discriminatory; inclusive and accessible |
| 3 | **Security and Safety** | Robust, secure, safe throughout lifecycle |
| 4 | **Human-Centricity** | Respect human rights; human oversight |
| 5 | **Privacy and Data Governance** | Protect personal data; quality data management |
| 6 | **Accountability and Integrity** | Clear governance; ethical conduct |
| 7 | **Sustainability** | Environmental and societal sustainability |

## ASEAN Member States — AI Maturity

| Country | AI Framework Status | Data Protection Law |
| --------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| Singapore | Model AIGF 2nd Ed + AI Verify (mature) | PDPA (2012, amended 2020) |
| Thailand | National AI Ethics Guidelines (2022) | PDPA (2019, effective 2022) |
| Indonesia | National AI Strategy (2020) | PDP Law (2022) |
| Malaysia | National AI Roadmap (2021–2025) | PDPA (2010) |
| Philippines | National AI Strategy (2021) | Data Privacy Act (2012) |
| Vietnam | National AI Strategy (2021) | Cybersecurity Law (2018) + PDP Decree (2023) |
| Brunei | Developing | No comprehensive data protection law |
| Cambodia | Developing | Draft data protection law |
| Laos | Developing | No comprehensive data protection law |
| Myanmar | Developing | No comprehensive data protection law |

## Cross-Border Considerations

ASEAN Digital Framework Agreement (DEFA, signed 2025):

- Aims to harmonise digital trade and data flows across ASEAN
- Data transfer provisions — working toward ASEAN data transfer mechanisms
- Mutual recognition of digital standards

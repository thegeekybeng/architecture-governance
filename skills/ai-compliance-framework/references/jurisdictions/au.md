# Australia — AI Ethics Framework

> **Status:** Active guidance (voluntary)
> **Binding:** NO — voluntary principles; Privacy Act 1988 is binding for data protection
> **Confidence:** HIGH
> **Source:** <https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/australias-artificial-intelligence-ethics-framework>

## Eight AI Ethics Principles

| # | Principle | Key Requirement |
| --- | ----------- | ---------------- |
| 1 | **Human, societal, and environmental wellbeing** | AI should benefit individuals, society, and environment |
| 2 | **Human-centred values** | Respect human rights, diversity, autonomy |
| 3 | **Fairness** | Inclusive, accessible, no unfair discrimination |
| 4 | **Privacy protection and security** | Respect privacy, secure data handling |
| 5 | **Reliability and safety** | Operate reliably, minimise risk |
| 6 | **Transparency and explainability** | Appropriate transparency about AI use and decisions |
| 7 | **Contestability** | Timely process to challenge AI decisions |
| 8 | **Accountability** | Identifiable responsible parties throughout lifecycle |

## Data Protection: Privacy Act 1988

- 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)
- APP 3: Collection of personal information — only what is reasonably necessary
- APP 6: Use/disclosure — only for purpose of collection (or related purpose)
- APP 11: Security of personal information
- APP 12: Access to personal information
- APP 13: Correction of personal information
- **Notifiable Data Breaches scheme** — mandatory notification to OAIC within 30 days

## Checklist

```text
[ ] Eight AI Ethics Principles self-assessment completed
[ ] Privacy Act compliance verified (APPs)
[ ] Contestability mechanism implemented
[ ] Transparency measures proportionate to risk
[ ] Fairness testing conducted
[ ] Responsible party identified for each AI system
```

# Brazil — AI Regulatory Framework (PL 2338/2023)

> **Status:** Draft legislation — approved by Senate, under House review
> **Binding:** NOT YET — LGPD (data protection) is binding
> **Confidence:** MEDIUM — bill subject to amendment during legislative process; verify current status
> **Sources:**
>
> - PL 2338/2023 (AI Regulatory Framework bill)
> - LGPD — Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (General Data Protection Law, 2018/2020)

## PL 2338/2023 Key Provisions (as proposed)

### Risk-Based Classification (inspired by EU AI Act)

| Risk Level | Description | Examples |
| ----------- | ------------- | --------- |
| **Excessive** | Prohibited AI uses | Social scoring by government, subliminal manipulation, exploitation of vulnerabilities |
| **High** | Strict obligations | Biometrics, criminal justice, employment, credit, healthcare, critical infrastructure, autonomous vehicles |
| **Non-high** | Lighter obligations | General-purpose AI, low-risk applications |

### High-Risk AI Requirements

- Risk assessment and mitigation
- Data governance measures
- Transparency and explainability
- Human oversight mechanisms
- Documentation and record-keeping
- Regular auditing

### Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA)

Required for high-risk AI systems — assessment of potential impacts on:

- Non-discrimination and equality
- Privacy and data protection
- Freedom of expression
- Consumer protection
- Due process

### AI Governance Authority

- Designated competent authority for AI regulation (likely ANPD — National Data Protection Authority, or new body)
- Powers: Regulation, supervision, enforcement, sandboxes

### Penalties

- Warning with compliance deadline
- Fines up to 2% of revenue (capped at R$50 million per infraction)
- Temporary or permanent suspension of AI system operation

## LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados) — Binding

> **Status:** Fully enforced since August 2020
> **Confidence:** HIGH
> **Regulator:** ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados)

Brazil's LGPD is heavily modelled on GDPR:

- **Legal basis required:** Consent, legitimate interest, contract, legal obligation, public policy, research, health protection, credit protection
- **Art. 20:** Right to review of automated decisions — data subjects can request review of decisions made solely by automated processing that affects their interests
- **Art. 20 §1:** Controller must provide clear information about criteria and procedures used in automated decisions
- **Sensitive data:** Race, ethnicity, religion, political opinion, union membership, health, sex life, genetics, biometrics — explicit consent required
- **DPO (Encarregado):** Must be appointed
- **DPIA (RIPD):** Required for high-risk processing
- **Cross-border transfer:** Adequate protection, standard clauses, or consent
- **Penalties:** Up to 2% of revenue (capped at R$50 million)

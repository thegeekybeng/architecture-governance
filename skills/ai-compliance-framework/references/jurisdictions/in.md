# India — NITI Aayog Responsible AI Principles

> **Status:** Enforced — DPDP Act enacted 2023; DPDP Rules notified Nov 14, 2025 (phased to May 2027)
> **Binding:** YES — DPDP Act and Rules are binding; NITI Aayog principles are voluntary
> **Confidence:** HIGH for DPDP Act; MEDIUM for AI-specific governance (evolving rapidly)
> **Sources:**
>
> - NITI Aayog: Responsible AI for All (2021)
> - NITI Aayog: Approach Document for India Part 1 & 2 (2021)
> - Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023)
> - IT Act 2000 (amended 2008)

## NITI Aayog — Seven Responsible AI Principles

| # | Principle | Description |
| --- | ----------- | ------------- |
| 1 | **Safety and Reliability** | AI systems must be safe, reliable, and perform as intended |
| 2 | **Equality** | AI must not discriminate based on caste, gender, religion, or socioeconomic status |
| 3 | **Inclusivity and Non-Discrimination** | Designed for diverse populations, including rural and underserved communities |
| 4 | **Privacy and Security** | Protect personal data and ensure cybersecurity |
| 5 | **Transparency and Explainability** | AI processes and decisions must be understandable |
| 6 | **Accountability** | Clear responsibility for AI outcomes |
| 7 | **Reinforce Positive Human Values** | AI should promote well-being and align with democratic values |

## Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023)

> **Status:** Enforced — DPDP Rules notified Nov 14, 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E))
> **Confidence:** HIGH — gazette-notified rules with phased commencement
> **Commencement:** Board provisions immediate; consent managers from 13 Nov 2026; all substantive obligations from 13 May 2027

**Key provisions:**

- **Consent-based processing:** Lawful purpose + individual consent required
- **Data Fiduciary obligations:** Purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation, accuracy
- **Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF):** Government may designate entities as SDFs with enhanced obligations:
  - Appoint Data Protection Officer (DPO)
  - Appoint independent Data Auditor
  - Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)
  - Comply with additional data localisation requirements
- **Data Principal rights:** Right to information, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, nominate
- **Children's data:** Verifiable parental consent required; no tracking/behavioural monitoring of children
- **Cross-border transfer:** Permitted except to countries specifically restricted by government notification
- **Penalties:** Up to ₹250 crore (~$30M USD) per breach
- **Data Protection Board of India:** Adjudicatory body for complaints and enforcement

## India AI Mission (2024)

Government initiative to:

- Build sovereign AI compute infrastructure (10,000+ GPU cluster)
- Create India-specific foundational models
- Establish AI safety and ethics standards
- Develop AI applications for government services (agriculture, healthcare, education, governance)

## Sector-Specific Considerations

| Sector | Regulator | AI Relevance |
| -------- | ----------- | ------------- |
| Financial Services | RBI (Reserve Bank of India) | ML model risk, digital lending guidelines, KYC automation |
| Healthcare | CDSCO, NMC | Clinical decision support, telemedicine AI |
| Telecom | TRAI, DoT | AI in network management, customer analytics |
| Government | MeitY | AI for governance, digital public infrastructure |

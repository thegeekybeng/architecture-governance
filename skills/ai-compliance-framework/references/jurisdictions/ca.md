# Canada — AIDA (Artificial Intelligence and Data Act)

> **Status:** Draft legislation — introduced as Part 3 of Bill C-27 (Digital Charter Implementation Act)
> **Binding:** NOT YET — Bill C-27 has not received Royal Assent as of this writing
> **Confidence:** MEDIUM — bill is subject to amendment during parliamentary process; verify current status
> **Source:** <https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovation-better-canada/en/artificial-intelligence-and-data-act>

## AIDA Key Provisions (as proposed)

### High-Impact AI Systems

- Definition based on potential impact on health, safety, human rights, or interests of individuals
- Specific criteria to be defined in regulations (not yet published)

### Requirements for High-Impact AI Systems

1. **Risk assessment** before making system available
2. **Mitigation measures** to address identified risks
3. **Monitoring** of compliance with mitigation measures
4. **Record-keeping** of assessments, measures, and monitoring
5. **Notification** to the AI Commissioner of serious harm
6. **Transparency** — publish plain-language description of the system

### AI and Data Commissioner

- New regulatory body to oversee AIDA compliance
- Powers to audit, investigate, and enforce
- Can issue compliance orders and recommend penalties

### Penalties

- Up to CAD $10 million or 3% of global gross revenue for non-compliance
- Up to CAD $25 million or 5% for reckless/knowing contravention causing serious harm

### Voluntary Code of Conduct

- Minister may publish codes of conduct for responsible AI
- Organisations can voluntarily commit to codes

## Data Protection: PIPEDA / Provincial Laws

- PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act) — federal private-sector privacy law
- 10 Fair Information Principles (consent, purpose, accuracy, safeguards, etc.)
- Provincial equivalents: Quebec Law 25, Alberta PIPA, BC PIPA
- **Quebec Law 25 (2023):** Automated decision-making provisions — right to be informed, right to explanation
- Mandatory breach reporting to OPC (Office of the Privacy Commissioner)
- Cross-border transfer: Adequate level of protection required

## Treasury Board Directive on Automated Decision-Making (Federal Government)

> **Status:** Binding for federal government institutions
> **Source:** <https://www.tbs-sct.canada.ca/pol/doc-eng.aspx?id=32592>

Requirements for federal AI systems:

- **Algorithmic Impact Assessment (AIA)** — mandatory before deployment
- Impact levels I–IV determine requirements (peer review, notice, explanation, human oversight)
- Open-source AIA tool available: <https://open.canada.ca/aia-eia-js/>
- Results must be published on Open Government Portal

| Impact Level | Peer Review | Notice | Explanation | Human Review | Audit Trail |
| ------------- | ------------ | -------- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| I (Low) | No | No | No | No | Yes |
| II | No | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| III | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| IV (Critical) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes (before decision) | Yes |
